# SpongeBob SquarePants: Titans of the Tide (`0100A0902789A000`)

1.4.0 from a merged `(1G+1U)` cartridge, SDK 20.5.6. Unreal Engine 5.x (IoStore archives) on NVN, modules `main`,
`subsdk0` and `sdk`. 19,563 shaders, 2,128 of them compute. Played on an iPhone 16 Pro: the menu, the first level,
audio and a save, at 30 fps (frame time p50 33.4 ms), 30 minutes without a crash.

## What it needs

- **Nothing in `patches.py`.** The engine's `FMallocBinned3` is found by the rewriter's engine-wide search
  (`dm/tools/ue_malloc.py`, #635): the UTF-16 `Binned3` name, its getter, the vtable slot and the constructor call are
  located in `main`, the call is cut and the host allocator takes its place. This is the first game here whose entry
  came from that search rather than a hand-written cut.
- **An engine region of 4064 MB**: `-SwitchVirtualAddressSpaceSize=4064` (`game_args`). Without it `nninitStartup`
  aborts in the SDK; **with 4096 it silently skips the region** (no `[mem] address region` line at all) and then reads
  a null manager at `main+0x14b922c`, and with 2048 the page allocator runs out of address space after about 13
  minutes (`nn::diag` abort at `main+0x11cc`, after 64 GB had been through the residency set). 4064 is the value
  FINAL FANTASY RESONANCE's profile uses.
- **The memory partition**: `mem_reserve 0x200000000` (8 GB), `mem_alias 0x180000000` (6 GB), `mem_stack
  0x10000000`, `app_pool 0xCD500000`, `sys_resource 0x1400000`, and `stdalloc_cap 0x80000000` (2 GB) so the engine's
  region and the SDK's own StandardAllocator region both fit in the alias region. The provisional guess (66 GB of
  reserve) cannot be mapped on iOS at all (`FATAL reserve guest AS`).

| key | value |
|---|---|
| `mem_reserve` | `0x200000000` (8 GB) |
| `mem_alias` | `0x180000000` (6 GB) |
| `mem_stack` | `0x10000000` |
| `app_pool` | `0xCD500000` |
| `sys_resource` | `0x1400000` |
| `game_args` | `-SwitchVirtualAddressSpaceSize=4064` |
| `stdalloc_cap` | `0x80000000` (2 GB) |

## Frames

30 fps: `frame time p50 33.41 ms / p95 34.83 ms` over 300-frame windows, which is the game's own pacing. 1,173 draws a
frame in the first level; the GPU is the limiter there (long frames 43–50 ms when a new area streams in).

1884; variation pairs 16804`.

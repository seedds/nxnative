# Batman: Arkham Knight (`0100ACD0163D0000`)

Update 1.0.5 (v327680) over the base game (the base NSP is 7 MB; the update holds the whole game, 26.9 GB), SDK 14.3.0 (its own `sdk` build). Part of Batman: Arkham Trilogy. Rocksteady's Unreal Engine 3: loose cooked packages (`BmGame/CookedSwitch/*.xxx`, Oodle-compressed chunks), texture caches (`*.tfc`), NVN, Wwise sound, CRI USM movies and Scaleform GFx menus. Modules `main` and `sdk`. GitHub issue #115.

Status: **in bring-up, not counted as playable.** On the Mac the game boots, plays its logo movies, draws its menus, the title and the main menu, and plays from a new story through the opening cutscenes to the cremation: 5 of 5 route runs reached frame 6,000 without a freeze since the task queue's barriers ([Open](#open)). Pauli's Diner, the first playable scene, loads and runs at 30 fps but was only played briefly (the `diner` route of [`bench.tsv`](bench.tsv)), so the Mac gameplay test that would count it as playable is still to do. 30 fps as on the Switch by default; 60 and 120 with `present_interval display` and `DM_FPS_TARGET` ([Frame rate](#frame-rate)). About 2.5–3 GB resident, an Unreal heap of 1.75 GB at the title. Not tried on an iPhone.

## Memory

- **Resolved: `err::ShowError Result=0x1803`, then `0xe03` and a crash at `main+0xb2dae8` during the static constructors.** The FMalloc getter (`main+0x192eb30`) constructs a static FMallocBinned3 (`main+0x51818c0`) inline on the first allocation: `AllocateAddressRegion` of 1 GB (`main+0x192eee8`, its small-block metadata), then 37 GB (`main+0x192fe50`, 74 pools of 512 MB). The cut jumps from the guard over the construction to the guard's release; the host has put its allocator's vtable in the object before any guest code runs. Unreal Engine 3's FMalloc takes 32-bit sizes and alignments; slots in `hle_uemalloc.c` (layout `UE3`), named through the thread-safe proxy's vtable (`main+0x3450158`). Nothing outside the allocator reads its pool base (`main+0x3fd1088`).
- The SDK 14.3.0 build's AddressRegionManager has the 1 GB node area (`sdk+0x12d8a0`: `mov w8/w9, #0x3fe00000` at `+0x12d8b0` / `+0x12d94c`) and aligns regions of 1 GB and more to 1 GB (`VammManager::AllocateAddressRegion`, `sdk+0x12d9b0`; the `mov w10, #0x40000000` at `+0x12daac`). Neither is cut: with the host allocator the game asks for no region that needs it so far.
- At the title: about 2.4 GB resident, 1.4 GB of Metal allocations, the Unreal heap at 1.75 GB.

## Files and shaders

- 4,093 cooked packages (`.xxx`, 19 GB): an uncompressed summary in Rocksteady's own serialization, then compressed chunks (tag `0x9E2A83C1`, 128 KB blocks, Oodle Mermaid). The game's `OodleLZ_Decompress` is `main+0x8f8250`, found by its name string.
- Shaders: `GlobalShaderCache-Switch.bin` (magic `BMSG`, stored whole) and `CombinedShaderCache-Switch.xxx` (29 MB, 136 MB decoded); no other package holds any. 24,721 records, 21,923 keys with `shader_key_skip` (2,564 vertex, 19,233 pixel, 124 compute, 2 geometry), all built for the Mac (28 minutes with 4 workers). With `0x7d0+0x18` the opening cutscenes still met shaders whose control sections differed from +0x778 on; with `0x770+0x78` all 40 dumped are in the map.
- Movies: CRI USM (`BmGame/Movies`, 7.5 GB), decoded by the game's own CRI threads (`CRI MPV Worker`); the logo movies play.

## Rendering

- The title screen renders a 3D scene (the deferred G-buffer at 1440×812: scene colour RG11B10F, normals RGB10A2, two RGBA8 targets), tiled lighting and post-processing in compute shaders (subgroup votes, ballots and shuffles, threadgroup atomics), a tone-mapping compute pass (1×1 R16F eye adaptation, a 16³ colour-grading LUT in a 256×16 RGBA8 target), a copy to the 1920×1080 target (`f93e9daa6c80f2c2`, with gamma and fade constants) and Scaleform's UI (`648ee7c01ac61562` + `45c7c510cd31e73a`).
- **Resolved: the tone-mapped scene was black.** Its colour-grading LUT (a 256×16 RGBA8 texture), like the other textures the game fills with the CPU, stayed empty until `tex_flush_upload` and `tex_dirty_upload` (with the first alone it still read zeros). With both, the title shows Batman; the scene colour then reaches 2.6–3.9 (RG11B10F), the 1×1 R16F eye adaptation reads 0.77 and the tone-mapped image is dark (mean 0.02).

## Frame rate

- The game asks for present interval 2 (30 fps) and caps itself with Unreal Engine 3's `MaxSmoothedFrameRate` (62, GEngine + 0x884); it steps by real time (`GDeltaTime` read 0.0333 at 30 fps, 0.0166 at 60; the fixed-step and "massaged" time-step globals `main+0x4840464` / `main+0x4840468` stay 0). `present_interval display` with `DM_FPS_TARGET=60` runs at 60; `DM_FPS_TARGET=120 DM_PRESENT_HZ=120 DM_PRESENT_LINK=2` raises the cap to 122 ([Unreal Engine 3's smoothed frame rate cap](../../../docs/reference/frame-rate.md#unreal-engine-3s-smoothed-frame-rate-cap)) and runs at 120 ([One frame per tick of a 120 Hz display link](../../../docs/reference/frame-rate.md#one-frame-per-tick-of-a-120-hz-display-link)).
- **Game speed** at 60 and 120: the same route (presses at fixed seconds) shows the same moment of the opening cutscene at the same time in the 60 and the 120 fps run (140 s: the Joker close-up; 150–180 s: the cremation prompt), and the cremation prompt comes 150–160 s after boot at 30 fps as well. Audio sync and physics are not checked separately (the runs at 60 and 120 had sound off or the device silent).
- **Measured on an M2 Max** (window off, display on, the opening cutscene at the Joker, 1440×812 G-buffer, 1080p output), before the CPU-written texture set fix (pull request #321): 60 fps held (frame p50 16.7 / p95 17.3 ms, 0–3 dropped in 300, on-screen p99 16.67 ms), at a 120 target only 83 fps (render thread bound: submission 11.3 ms of CPU a frame). After it: 119.8 fps, frame p50 8.35 / p95 8.9 ms, frame interval p99 9.7 ms, submission 2.2 ms, GPU 8.2 ms a frame; the GPU is the limit now. The diner scene took 7.8 ms of GPU a frame at 30 fps in the user's run, so 120 there needs a lower render size or MetalFX upscaling, not measured yet (the lid was closed for the rest of the night).
- At the time of the 120 fps run the display was off and the display link silent, so the on-screen statistics are missing for that run; a run with the lid closed (no screen at all) stalls for seconds at a time in the presents (nextDrawable) and the movies: no measurement with the lid closed.

## Mac routes

## Open

- **Resolved: white and light grey layers over the menus and the title, the menu text missing.** Scaleform's vertex streams sit at addresses 2 mod 4, which Metal reads from the aligned address below: the shapes' positions became garbage, and the shapes (white with alpha 0.2–0.7 by their colour transforms) covered the screen many times over. The host copies such streams: Vertex streams at addresses that are not 4-byte aligned (pull request #319). On the way, ruled out: Metal fast math (all shaders rebuilt with `-fno-fast-math`), the deferred uniform updates, stencil masks, and the canvas quad drawn first (a 4×4 white texture with vertex colour (1, 1, 1, 0): its colour is the vertex's third attribute, black). Eden showed the same white on this Mac, probably for the same reason under MoltenVK.
- **Resolved: the freezes** at the menus (around frames 1,200–1,800), in the opening (frame 4,200, the first scene of the cremation) and in Pauli's Diner (the user's run, frame 7,090): the picture stopped and stayed, sound went on. Every task worker waited on its own event, the rendering thread on a task's event (`main+0xbdd9f4` from `main+0x165de24` / `main+0x165decc`), the main thread polled the render fence. In the frozen process (lldb) the task had state 3 and one of its four subtasks (+0x48..+0x60) unfinished, flagged queued, in no queue (head = tail = 0x438): the task ring's push and pop had no barriers, and a worker on Apple's cores dropped the item ([Lock-free queues published without barriers](../../../docs/reference/threads.md#lock-free-queues-published-without-barriers)). With the barrier cuts 5 of 5 runs from the user's save through the menus and the opening reached frame 6,000; before, 2 of 3 froze (r1 at 4,200, r3 at 1,500).
- **Open (worked around in the host; the cause is a race in the game's own code): a rare crash** at `main+0x2031740` (sig 11, `lr=main+0xeadc0`), once in about 30 boots, always about 38 s after start, at the end of the logo movie, on the rendering thread. Scaleform Video's image (vtable `main+0x35f0900`) keeps a plain pointer to its player (vtable `main+0x35f0448`, 0x550 bytes, a host block of its own) at +0x38; the rendering thread loads it and calls the player's slot 0x100 under a lock the main thread does not take when the video provider's destructor (`main+0x202a700`) clears the pointer and destroys the player. Probes in four runs: one player per boot (made at 0.8–1.5 s, deleted at 38.6–40.4 s on the main thread), its image updated by the rendering thread once a frame up to 38 ms before the deletion. The workaround: only that class's freed blocks wait 250 ms before the host zone reuses them, so a late call still finds the player's vtable; the race itself is untouched and stays open (a fix would have to be a rewriter cut that takes the lock in the destructor, not tried): [Freed Unreal heap blocks kept a moment before reuse](../../../docs/reference/memory.md#freed-unreal-heap-blocks-kept-a-moment-before-reuse).
- **Pauli's Diner: loads and runs, only played briefly.** The `diner` route of [`bench.tsv`](bench.tsv) (the user's save, boot to the cremation, holding B, a few steps and camera turns as Officer Owens) reaches the diner at 30 fps on an M2 Max: frame p50 33.4 ms, GPU p50 14–18 ms a frame over the 75 s window, no freeze, no crash, no shader missing from the map (2026-10-26, chain `a4e2088c2e69`). Open there: four shaders sample a 2D texture through a slot they declare as a 2D array (`texture type mismatch` red lines, `e4bd7b26b4a2dc5b`, `553c51835a24a36a`, `fad24ea404d1bbac`, `26ccc3148f457613`; bound as is, no visible fault in the screenshots), the 2–3 frames over 100 ms while the diner streams in, and play beyond the first minutes. Window runs need focus: a window that loses it pauses the game ([below](#open)); `DM_OE_SCRIPT=focus=1@5,focus=1@10,…` keeps a scripted run going. The route by seconds, for runs without `bench.py`: `DM_KEYS=51:A,64:A,78:A,91:A,104:A,118:A` reaches the cremation prompt at about 150 s at 60 fps (the frame-numbered `f1500:A…f3500:A` at 30 fps); hold B there (`B~10`), then walk (`LUp`).
- **120 fps in the diner and later:** GPU bound at the native size; MetalFX present upscaling (`DM_PRESENT_UPSCALE=metalfx`) and fullscreen not measured yet.
- **A window that loses focus pauses the game** (`oe focus state → 2`, the game sets focus handling mode 1): Mac runs driven by `DM_CTL` should run without a window.
- **iPhone:** not built. Memory needs checking first (2.4 GB resident and a 1.75 GB Unreal heap at the title); the shader libraries for iOS are not built.
- Arkham City (`01003F00163CE000`) and Arkham Asylum (`0100E870163CA000`), also asked for in #115, are not started.

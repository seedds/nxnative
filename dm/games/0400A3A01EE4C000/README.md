# Pokémon Pokopia (`0400A3A01EE4C000`)

A Switch 2 game: update v655360 (the title screen says Ver. 2.0.1), SDK 20.5.25 built for the Switch 2 (`Ounce`). Koei Tecmo's KTGL engine on NVN2, modules `main`, `subsdk0` (WebM movies) and `sdk`. **Plays on the Mac**: the opening, the character choice and the first area's tutorial at about 60 fps, including 31 minutes of the user's playtest with no GPU error and no crash. A save from an earlier run loads and continues. **Plays on the iPhone** ([iPhone](#iphone)): a 19-minute playtest on an iPhone 17 Pro from a new game into the first area at about 60 fps, with no crash; at 60 fps pipeline builds stall it for up to 7.5 s, while a short session at 30 fps (`Documents/env.txt`) held its rate with no audio underrun (open).

## What it needs

| Setting | Where | Why |
|---|---|---|
| `mem_reserve 0x270000000`, `app_pool 0x240000000` | [`game.tsv`](game.tsv) | Donkey Kong Bananza's 9 GB layout, not measured here. The NPDM declares no system resource |
| `mem_used 0x5600000` | `game.tsv` | the rewritten modules take 82 MB, more than the default 64 MB that `SetHeapSize` checks the game's request against |
| `pad_layout full` | `game.tsv` | |
| `depth_mode zero` | `game.tsv` | the game sets near = 0 (the log's `depth mode` line). Without the key the pipeline archive made offline holds the other entry point, and the host refuses it |

## Files

- A dump folder whose `updates/<version>/` holds the whole RomFS; [`pack_dump.py`](../../../scripts/pack_dump.py) packs it without a base RomFS. RomFS: the newer RDB (as ROMANCE OF THE THREE KINGDOMS 8 REMAKE's) under `motor_asset_re/KIDS_PLATFORM_OUNCE`, and a `LD_C0_R.BIN` / `LI_C0_R.IDX` LINKDATA pair.
- Shaders: 131,426 in [`shader_map.tsv`](shader_map.tsv) ([KTGL on NVN2](../../../docs/reference/shaders.md#ktgl-on-nvn2-switch-2-glslc-outputs-and-ld_--li_-linkdata)). The Mac playtest's library held only the 8,157 that its runs asked for (with their pixel shaders), listed in [`shader_seen.tsv`](shader_seen.tsv); the NSP script's `--seen-shaders` builds those alone ([Building selected Mac shaders](../../../docs/development.md#building-selected-mac-shaders)). Such a library has no stamp and no `c1_full.tsv`, and each start logs that in red. On chain `20b9b1c2e4e4` an M4 Pro with other jobs running took 18 minutes for the 8,158 shaders of the list and the last logs (Metal's compile cache held nearly all of its libraries: 113 compiled), or 16 minutes with all of them cached, then about 5 minutes for the 7.9 GB Mac app. That app boots to walking at about 16.7 ms a frame with no GPU error and no shader reported missing, and later areas are untested on it. The NSP script builds all 131,426: an M3 Max compiled them for the iPhone in about 2 h 26 min with nothing cached (chain `d24f05cf9d78`; the estimate from 1,001 shaders had been 2.5 hours on an M4 Pro), since [the builder stopped keeping the 4.4 GB of packages in memory](../../../docs/reference/shaders.md#a-shader-build-that-keeps-its-packages-in-memory-runs-one-worker-at-a-time) and [its compiles stopped instantiating Metal's texture classes again](../../../docs/reference/shaders.md#each-metal-compile-instantiates-metals-texture-classes-again) and [its Python stopped limiting it](../../../docs/reference/shaders.md#rewrites-that-try-every-character-of-a-large-source) (before all of these, players saw about one shader a second and ETAs of 20 to 200 hours: GitHub issue #1036). The map's 105,534 pixel shaders each have `_ba` and `_bl` libraries, 57% of its compiles before those whose MSL matches another's, while the runs on the 8,157-shader library opened none of them (file access times; 7,985 of its 8,157 `_b` libraries were opened). Those runs only reached the first area (open).
- [`pso_manifest.tsv`](pso_manifest.tsv) is the empty placeholder: no seed list has been recorded. The IPA needs the file (`build_ipa.sh` copies it into the app and stopped at `copy shader tables` without it), and with it a start logs `0 lines` instead of a red line for a missing list. In the runs here no draw waited on it.

## Shaders

- Resolved: `cc679c7b4d3e703c` did not translate (`LDC with a bindless bank or an address mode`): an indexed read of c1 with the bank in the address register, found on Hyrule Warriors: Age of Imprisonment, which ships the same shader ([LDC with the bank in its address register](../../../docs/reference/shaders.md#ldc-with-the-bank-in-its-address-register-is)); all 8,158 shaders of the playtest library now translate, and the walk below logged no shader failing to load. The census also lists f55 three times, two more of that LDC and 16 BRX with no target: whether they are the forms since translated ([Barrier moves around warp shuffles](../../../docs/reference/shaders.md#barrier-moves-around-warp-shuffles)) is not checked (open).
- A `3D binding fallback` line fires once at about frame 2100: a 2D 8x8 texture in a 3D slot. The shader is not identified (open).

## Rendering

- Frames take about 16.7 ms. A cold file cache, as after a reboot, keeps the game on the loading icon for over a minute, and a clean run there says nothing: check that `[nvn] frame N: … calls` reached about 3,500.
- The lit HDR target is RG11B10, exposure a 2x1 RGBA32F image, tone map `075fd7285c64b798` with a 32³ LUT, final `ed5f4c6386ca621d`, the luminance histogram compute `2118ab534fac88c6`.
- `texraw` pads rows: an RG11B10 dump has a 16-byte stride with 4-byte pixels at the head of each row. It returns one slice of a 3D texture, and serials shift between runs.

## iPhone

- **iPhone 17 Pro** (`iPhone18,1`, iOS 27.2.1 `24B156`, 2026-11-12; Xcode 27.2, a free Personal Team, `com.apple.developer.kernel.increased-memory-limit`): a 19-minute playtest (64,800 frames) from a new game through the character choice into the first area, with no crash and no red line. The game wrote its save (11 commits); loading it is not checked. Per 300 frames the frame time's p50 was 16.6 to 17.9 ms and the GPU's 6 to 17.5 ms. The phone reached thermal state 2 (serious) after 4 min 48 s and left it after 14 min 13 s; from about 6.5 minutes the p95 was 26 to 30 ms, most of it frames waiting in submit and present while the GPU used nearly the whole frame.
- Stalls: pipelines built on the submit thread stopped the game 56 times for 100 ms or more, 40 s in all; the longest took 7.5 s (63 pipelines and 38 shader libraries) and 7.0 s (11 pipelines). Audio ran dry and input waited through them (75 audio blocks with underruns). With Metal 4 pipelines off a draw waits for its pipeline (`hybrid pipelines off`). After the session `Documents/pso_user.tsv` held 2,674 lines, which the next start prepares before the first frame. A 9.8 s frame at the character choice is the software keyboard for the name, not a stall.
- The build: the dump folder with `--platform ios --prepare-xcode`, on commit `ccd834e0` (shader chain `d24f05cf9d78`), with all 131,426 iOS shaders, installed from Xcode (about 8 minutes over USB 3, 23 GB sent). The app takes 39.1 GB on the phone, 31.7 GB of it the shader library as one file. The unsigned IPA of the same build is 25.4 GB.
- Settings: the defaults with Handheld (`DM_OPMODE=0`) and the Metal performance HUD; Low Power Mode off; an 8BitDo Ultimate 2 Wireless controller.
- Metal 4 pipelines (a second session, `DM_PSO_MTL4=1` with the pipeline prebuild): the first start built the 17,952 pairs' bases in 732.6 s before the first frame, as 11 archive files of about 150 MB, and the phone reached thermal state 2 after 538 s, still in the prebuild. In play no frame took 2 s (the longest 1.3 s; 21 of 100 ms or more, against 80 in the first session), but none of the app's CPU time ran on performance cores, and 600 to 1,970 of each 2,000 audio callbacks underran. About 3 minutes in, iOS reset Bluetooth (a `BT XPC Timeout` stackshot), which dropped the controller and the headphones; at that moment the system had 26 MB free and 5.5 GB wired, and 0.47 GB of the guest's file pages were resident, against about 2.5 GB in the first session.
- 30 fps: `DM_PRESENT_INTERVAL=display` and `DM_FPS_TARGET=30` in `Documents/env.txt`, Metal 4 off (the profile offers no frame-rate setting); the host logs `present cap 30 fps (DM_FPS_TARGET) applied by the present interval alone`. A short session (1,800 frames, about 1.5 minutes) held 30 fps (per 300 frames the frame time's p95 was 34.2 to 36.4 ms, the GPU's p50 2.5 to 26 ms) at thermal state 0, with 67 to 78% of the CPU time on performance cores and no audio underrun; the player heard no stutter. The session before it, 12 minutes at 60 fps with Metal 4 off, ran on efficiency cores only from about 100 s on, at thermal state 1, with underruns in 19 of 29 audio blocks.
- The game steps by elapsed time, not per frame, so the cap keeps its speed (Mac, the same switches): from the same spot a 0.7 s stick hold covered at least as much ground at 30 fps as at 60. In 150 s headless runs the CPU energy fell from 2.4 W to 0.97 W and the GPU's busy time from about 700 to 516 ms a second (11.8 to 17.2 ms a frame, as its clock dropped). A 20-minute windowed session held 30 fps.
- Memory: at start the app had 7.97 GB to its memory limit, below the game's 9.00 GB pool, so the guest memory is in files (the `[mem] guest memory` line; [iOS ends the app once the guest's touched pages reach its memory limit](../../../docs/reference/memory.md#ios-ends-the-app-once-the-guests-touched-pages-reach-its-memory-limit)). In the session the footprint peaked at 1.6 GB, with up to 2.8 GB of guest pages dirty in memory and 3.97 GB of the files on disk.

## Driving it on the Mac

The first boot shows the language list: `A`, `LUp`, `A` picks English. The character choice takes `LLeft` before `A`. From a warm cache, `A A LLeft A A Plus` reaches walking in about 300 s. On later boots, `A` at the title screen a few times continues the save and reaches walking in about 110 s. The work folder has `run/boot.sh`, `run/hdrive.sh` (no window, timed control lines) and `run/spam.sh`. The packed app takes about a minute to reach the language list (14 s of it the prepare step on a second start), so the fixed times in `run/spam.sh` miss it: press the keys once the list shows.

## Open

- The picture has not been compared with a Switch 2. The user's playtest found it fine. Sunlit ground matches published iPhone shots of the game. Shaded ground and walls are lavender. The player carries fine contour lines, already present in colour target 5435, which may be the material. In some moving shots far blocks showed through near ones; this has not been rechecked since the fixes below.
- Only the first area has been played on this build. A new save written through the game has not been checked: the 31-minute playtest wrote none.
- The pipeline archive saved nothing on the walk from the language list to the first area, on the partial library: archive lookups found 0 and found none 655 times, and 216 bases were compiled, though the host's probe found the archive's bases. The draws' pairs seem not to be those of `shader_pairs.tsv`, which come from BNSH variations. Whether a full library differs is not checked.
- On the iPhone at 60 fps, pipelines built on the submit thread stall the game for seconds, and Metal 4 pipelines trade those stalls for a 12-minute first-start prebuild and starved audio on a hot phone ([iPhone](#iphone)). 30 fps through `Documents/env.txt` played without either in a short session; a longer run would show whether the profile should offer it (`present_interval display` with a 30 fps target) as a Frame rate setting. Frame buffering is untested.
